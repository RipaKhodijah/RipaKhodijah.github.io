I am a Physics graduate from Indonesia, specializing in Geophysics and Data Science. My academic and research background focuses heavily on volcanology, atmospheric science, and environmental monitoring using high-resolution spatial data analysis.

I am highly proficient in utilizing Remote Sensing, Geographic Information Systems (GIS), and Machine Learning techniques. My research experience includes analyzing lagged relationships between volcanic emissions and local weather parameters, with specific focus areas on major volcanic systems such as Mount Lewotobi Laki-laki and Mount Llaima. I regularly work with advanced data tools and platforms, including PlanetScope imagery and NASA Giovanni, to extract critical environmental insights.

In addition to my research experience, I have served as the Chairperson of Komunitas Hijau Fisika and gained extensive field experience through the "Seven Summit Volcanoes" environmental expedition. I am also an alumna of Bangkit Academy (Machine Learning Cohort), which has strengthened my expertise in applying data-driven models to physical and environmental phenomena.

Currently, I am looking to leverage my expertise in research engineering, spatial data analytics, and environmental science within professional and academic roles.
